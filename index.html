Retrieval & LLM systems

Most RAG demos stop at the pipeline. The interesting work is the failure modes: what the system does when retrieval comes back weak, and what it does when someone tries to talk it out of its rules.

  • Chunking, embeddings, FAISS retrieval
  • Confidence gating instead of hallucinating
  • Prompt-injection and jailbreak hardening
  • Hit rate@k and MRR on held-out eval sets

Computer Vision

Vision work with the evaluation attached. A deep model only counts if it beats the classical baseline I ran first, and per-class numbers go in the report next to the headline accuracy.

  • Content-based image retrieval (CLIP, ResNet, FAISS)
  • CNN classification and transfer learning
  • Classical baselines: HSV, HOG, ORB, K-Means
  • Per-class precision and recall, not just accuracy

Deployment & MLOps

A model on your laptop isn't shipped. I package models into services people can actually hit — with logging, versioning, and a rollback plan.

  • FastAPI inference endpoints
  • Dockerized services & CI
  • Hugging Face Spaces deployments
  • Monitoring & model versioning
